Podcasts & Interviews

  • The Blind Spot: Finding Our Way to Centre In Relationship. Catherine and Kara Nance discuss fierce compassion, how the law of three shows up in interpersonal relationships, and the importance of honoring each other's experiences.
  • Evolving Enneagram: Transmuting Anger into Love through Consent & Yielding. Evolving Enneagram founder Nhien Vuong interviews Catherine (Point 8) about her personal, spiritual journey with the Enneagram.
  • Enneagram Maine Interviews, hosted by Michael Naylor, a conversation with "a beautiful, introspective, and magnanimous Point Eight."
  • End of the Road Podcast: The Enneagram, Buddhism, and "Higher Mind." Catherine speaks about how they all tie together in her life.
  • The Blindspot, an Enneagram Podcast. Catherine on her SO/SX/SP instinctual stacking and her story as founder of The Awakened Company.
  • Going Conscious with Nikki Trott. How profit and purpose can exist together, and why we must first awaken ourselves.
  • It's All About Evolving. On The Awakened Company, Catherine's first business book, published by Namaste Publishing (Eckhart Tolle's publisher).
  • Kourting Everything. "Catherine Bell is transformative. Her work is life-changing for individuals and organizations." (Dr. Kortni Alston)
  • Calgary Business Podcast. Catherine talks about Awakening Business.
  • Sacred Changemakers with Jayne Warrilow. A deep conversation into the heart of Awakening.
  • Awakened Companies: Leading with Presence, Purpose, and People. Catherine on why focusing two-thirds on corporate culture and one-third on financial success is where the magic happens.
  • Conscious Leadership, the Enneagram & Building an Awakened Company. A conversation on healthy corporate cultures and leading as an Enneagram Point Eight.
  • Following Your Lightning-Bolt Knowing. Catherine on intuitive leadership and trusting inner knowing.

Podcast links point to each show's official page; some episode-specific links couldn't be independently verified and may lead to the show's main feed rather than the exact episode.
