In this inspiring and practical guide, Catherine R. Bell and Russ Hudson invite readers to unlock a powerful new doorway into creative life: the Nine Enneagram Points.

AI can draft the essay and render the image. What it cannot do is long, risk, wonder, suffer, awaken, or be changed by the act of making. As technology transforms what gets produced, this book returns creativity to its source: our humanity, our lived experience, and the particular way each of us meets the world.

Readers may know the Enneagram as a map of personality and presence. Here, Bell and Hudson reveal it as something more: a dynamic system of nine creative energies alive in every person. Each Point offers a distinct way of perceiving, shaping, collaborating, and bringing something new into being.

Bell, author of The Awakened Company and a leading voice in bringing the Enneagram into organizational life, has spent decades working with leaders, teams, and systems where creativity becomes real. Hudson, one of the Enneagram's foremost teachers and developers, brings a lifetime of insight into its spiritual and psychological depths. Together, they show readers how to access all nine energies, not as abstract Points, but as living forces that can deepen awareness, strengthen collaboration, and ignite original work.

Woven throughout are voices from creative contributors around the world (artists, poets, sculptors, musicians, fashion designers, arts leaders, business innovators, and authors) who speak from inside their own creative process and illuminate how these energies move in practice.

In The Creative Moment, readers will discover:

  • The creative characteristics of each of the nine Enneagram Points, and how to recognize which energies are most and least available in themselves.
  • The lessons each Point and Instinct offers for the creative process, personal growth, and spiritual development.
  • Practical tools for bringing each energy into balance and creating healthier, more generative collaborations.
  • A clear roadmap for using organizational process to move a creative project from vision to reality.
  • The expansive possibilities that emerge when all nine energies are awakened, integrated, and brought into creative problem-solving.
“Creativity in its fullness requires plugging in to a deeper part of our humanity,” the authors write. At a moment when so much can be automated, The Creative Moment offers both inspiration and method: a way to reclaim the inner power that cannot be replicated, and to bring that power into art, work, relationships, and the future we are creating together.
