Enneagram Coaching

Catherine has been involved with the Enneagram since she was about 15 years old, first introduced to the system by her mother and aunt. At first, she didn't want to be boxed into a Point. As her journey continued, she learned that the Enneagram is really about unlocking your dominant Point and developing and cultivating the other Points. It's a deep journey into our inner experience, illuminating how we engage with both the outer and secret parts of our personal experience. The Enneagram is one of the most transformational roadmaps for those who are ready for it.

Catherine is Riso-Hudson Enneagram Certified, holds an Enneagram coaching certification, is certified in TOLAT, studied TriType with Katherine and David Fauvre, and has studied with David Daniels, Ginger Lapid-Bogda, and Tom Condon. She holds an MBA from Queen's University, a sociology degree from Western University, and is a certified yoga instructor. She implements the Enneagram roadmap with executives who are truly ready to awaken.

What is the Enneagram?

The Enneagram is a profound personality roadmap with 9 essential Points to explore. It teaches us to cultivate our unique gifts, reveals our work-ons, our wounds, and our light, and maps out a specific path to help us develop into our potential.

Working with the Enneagram can help you develop more awareness of your physical senses, your emotional capacities, and your thinking patterns. It builds self-awareness, cultivates presence, and leads to a more enriched life: one where you can live into your potential.

Executive Coaching

Catherine's philosophy: We are all here to create. What shall we create today? A story, a play, a company, a delay. Let's bring the splinters of society together in the most real way, so that true soul and mind stream can show us the way forward: the way of interdependence between ourselves and others.

One on One

Catherine's aim is for everyone she works with to embrace their own empowerment (heart, head, and hands) and to truly live into their potential in a sustainable way. Working with Catherine is a journey into your own leading, longing, loving, quiet, hurting, breaking, and, of course, healing. It is not for the faint of heart.

Strategy (with Soul)

Organizations are the way we will solve the world's greatest challenges, and we need to work together differently. Catherine believes organizations are a force for good, offering opportunities for awakening "Strategies with Soul" by incorporating the measurement of culture into plans in a highly customized and powerful way, helping organizations to flourish.

Keynotes & Unique Creative Facilitation

Catherine designs and leads keynotes and facilitated sessions that go beyond a traditional talk, bringing a room into direct, embodied experience of the material rather than just hearing about it. Every session is customized to the audience, and can be delivered as a standalone keynote, a half or full day workshop, or woven into a longer offsite or retreat.

Popular session themes include:

  • Personal Vision. Helping individuals get clear on what they're here to create, and why.
  • Values. Uncovering the values that actually drive decisions, not just the ones written on the wall.
  • Awakening Leadership. Building the self-awareness and presence that let leaders show up as their fullest selves.
  • Awakening Relationships. Strengthening trust and honest communication between colleagues, partners, and teams.
  • Awakening Teams. Helping teams move from surface-level collaboration into genuine, high-trust partnership.
  • Awakening Creativity. Unlocking the creative energy available in every person and every organization, drawing on the Enneagram framework at the heart of The Creative Moment.
